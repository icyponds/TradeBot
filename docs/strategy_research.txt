1. VOLATILITY BREAKOUT STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
- Detect low volatility periods (Bollinger Band squeeze, ATR compression)
- Enter on breakout with volume confirmation
- Works well in crypto's regime-switching nature

Implementation:
- Monitor Bollinger Band width (BBW) or ATR percentile
- When BBW drops below 20th percentile, prepare for breakout
- Enter long on upper band break with volume > 1.5x average
- Enter short on lower band break with volume > 1.5x average
- Stop loss: Opposite band or 1 ATR

Pros:
- Catches big moves early
- Clear entry/exit rules
- Works across timeframes

Cons:
- False breakouts in choppy markets
- Requires patience during compression periods
- May miss gradual trends


2. ORDER FLOW IMBALANCE STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
- Use Hyperliquid's order book and trade data
- Detect aggressive buying/selling via trade tape analysis
- Enter when imbalance exceeds threshold

Implementation:
- Track buy vs sell volume over rolling windows (1m, 5m, 15m)
- Calculate Order Flow Imbalance (OFI) = (BuyVol - SellVol) / TotalVol
- Enter long when OFI > 0.6 across multiple timeframes
- Enter short when OFI < -0.6 across multiple timeframes
- Quick exits on imbalance reversal

Pros:
- Very short-term alpha potential
- Sub-minute signal generation
- Direct measure of market pressure

Cons:
- Requires fast execution
- High turnover, more fees
- May need WebSocket trade stream


3. CROSS-EXCHANGE BASIS TRADE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
- Compare Hyperliquid perp price vs spot prices on other exchanges
- Trade when basis deviates from historical norms
- Mean reversion on the basis spread

Implementation:
- Fetch spot prices from Binance/Coinbase via API
- Calculate basis = (Perp - Spot) / Spot * 100
- Track rolling mean and std of basis
- Enter when basis Z-score > 2 or < -2
- Exit when Z-score reverts to 0

Pros:
- Market-neutral strategy
- Statistically robust foundation
- Less correlated with directional strategies

Cons:
- Requires external data feeds (Binance, Coinbase APIs)
- Latency sensitive for execution
- Need to account for exchange-specific factors


4. VOLATILITY REGIME STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
- Classify market into volatility regimes (high/medium/low)
- Dynamically adjust strategy weights based on regime
- Mean reversion works in low vol, momentum in high vol

Implementation:
- Calculate realized volatility over 24h, 7d windows
- Compare to historical percentiles
- Low vol regime: Increase mean reversion weight
- High vol regime: Increase momentum weight, tighter stops
- Medium vol: Balanced allocation

Pros:
- Adapts to changing market conditions
- Reduces drawdowns during unfavorable regimes
- Meta-strategy that improves existing strategies

Cons:
- Regime detection has lag
- Transitions can be noisy
- Requires sufficient historical data


5. MACHINE LEARNING SIGNAL ENSEMBLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
- Combine outputs from existing strategies
- Train classifier on historical signal quality
- Weight signals by predicted accuracy

Implementation:
- Collect features: strategy signals, volatility, volume, time of day
- Label historical signals as profitable/unprofitable
- Train gradient boosting or neural network classifier
- Use predicted probability to weight position sizes
- Retrain periodically on new data

Pros:
- Potentially better signal quality
- Can discover non-linear relationships
- Adapts over time

Cons:
- Requires ML training infrastructure
- Risk of overfitting
- Needs significant historical data
- Black box nature makes debugging harder

IMPROVEMENTS TO EXISTING STRATEGIES
================================================================================

OU MEAN REVERSION STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current Implementation:
- Uses Z-score to detect deviation from mean
- Fixed entry/exit thresholds

Suggested Improvements:

1. Adaptive Z-Score Thresholds
   - Current: Fixed zscore_entry = 2.0
   - Improvement: Scale threshold by rolling volatility
   - Example: zscore_entry = 2.0 * (avg_vol / current_vol)
   - Rationale: Tighter thresholds in low vol, wider in high vol

2. Half-Life Filter
   - Only trade if estimated half-life < 24 hours
   - Half-life = -log(2) / log(theta) where theta is OU mean reversion speed
   - Rationale: Avoid slow mean-reverting assets

3. Regime Filter
   - Disable strategy when BTC is in strong trend (ADX > 30)
   - Rationale: Mean reversion fails in trending markets

4. Entry Timing
   - Wait for momentum to slow before entry (RSI divergence)
   - Rationale: Catch falling knives less often


MOMENTUM FACTOR STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current Implementation:
- Ranks assets by momentum score
- Enters top/bottom N assets
- Fixed rebalance periods

Suggested Improvements:

1. Event-Driven Rebalancing
   - Current: Rebalance on fixed schedule
   - Improvement: Rebalance when rank changes significantly
   - Example: If asset drops from top 3 to bottom 10, exit immediately
   - Rationale: React faster to momentum shifts

2. Momentum Crash Protection
   - Monitor correlation of momentum returns to BTC drawdowns
   - Reduce exposure when BTC drops > 5% in 24h
   - Rationale: Momentum strategies crash during market panics

3. Sector Neutrality
   - Ensure long/short balance within sectors (DeFi, L1, meme, etc.)
   - Rationale: Reduce sector concentration risk

4. Momentum Quality
   - Prefer smooth momentum over choppy (high R² of price trend)
   - Rationale: Smooth momentum more likely to persist


STATISTICAL ARBITRAGE STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current Implementation:
- Uses correlation to find related pairs
- Trades spread Z-score

Suggested Improvements:

1. Cointegration Testing
   - Current: Correlation-based pair selection
   - Improvement: Use Engle-Granger or Johansen cointegration test
   - Rationale: Cointegration is more robust than correlation

2. Dynamic Hedge Ratios
   - Current: Static hedge ratio
   - Improvement: Use Kalman filter for rolling hedge ratio
   - Rationale: Relationship between pairs drifts over time

3. Spread Stationarity Check
   - Regularly test if spread is still stationary (ADF test)
   - Exit if stationarity breaks down
   - Rationale: Non-stationary spreads can blow up

4. Transaction Cost Awareness
   - Factor in fees when calculating expected profit
   - Only trade if expected profit > 2x fees
   - Rationale: Stat arb has high turnover


FUNDING RATE ARBITRAGE STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current Implementation:
- Enters when funding rate exceeds threshold
- Delta-neutral long perp + short spot (or vice versa)

Suggested Improvements:

1. Adaptive Thresholds
   - Current: Fixed funding rate threshold
   - Improvement: Use funding rate percentiles
   - Example: Enter when funding > 90th percentile of 30-day distribution
   - Rationale: What's "high" changes over time

2. Funding Term Structure
   - Monitor predicted funding rates for future periods
   - Prefer positions where funding expected to persist
   - Rationale: Single high funding may not repeat

3. Cost-Adjusted APR
   - Calculate net APR after trading fees and slippage
   - Only enter if net APR > hurdle rate (e.g., 20% annualized)
   - Rationale: Gross funding can be misleading

4. Funding Rate Momentum
   - Track if funding is increasing or decreasing
   - Enter on high funding + increasing momentum
   - Rationale: Catch funding before it peaks

1. BACKTESTING FRAMEWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Include slippage modeling (0.1% per trade minimum)
- Account for funding payments in backtests
- Track per-strategy Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown
- Compare live vs backtest performance

2. STRATEGY WARMUP PERIOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Don't trade until sufficient data collected
- Example: Need 100 candles of 15m data = 25 hours
- Prevents trading on incomplete signals
- Log warmup status in dashboard

3. CORRELATION MONITORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Track correlation between strategy returns
- Alert if correlation > 0.7 (too similar)
- Reduce allocation to correlated strategies
- Goal: Diversification of return streams

4. EXECUTION OPTIMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Implement TWAP (Time-Weighted Average Price) for larger orders
- Split orders > $500 into 3-5 chunks over 30-60 seconds
- Reduce market impact and slippage
- Track execution quality vs arrival price

5. REAL-TIME P&L ATTRIBUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Track which strategy contributes to gains/losses
- Dashboard showing per-strategy contribution
- Disable underperforming strategies automatically
- Monthly strategy performance review
